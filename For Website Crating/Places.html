Places to Visit in Phuket

Hover on any picture and watch the info slide out ✨

Patong Beach

The most famous beach in Phuket! Soft white sand, clear water, water sports, and amazing sunsets. Perfect for both relaxing and having fun.

Patong Beach
★ Must Visit

Big Buddha ★

A giant white marble Buddha sitting high on a hill. One of the most peaceful and spiritual places in Phuket with an incredible 360° view of the whole island.

Big Buddha ★ Special

Phuket Old Town

Beautiful colorful Sino-Portuguese buildings, street art, cute cafés and local shops. Best place to walk around and take photos.

Phuket Old Town

Wat Chalong

The largest and most important Buddhist temple in Phuket. Stunning architecture, peaceful atmosphere and beautiful decorations.

Wat Chalong Temple

Phi Phi Islands

World-famous islands near Phuket with crystal clear water, dramatic limestone cliffs and incredible snorkeling spots. A must day-trip!

Phi Phi Islands

Freedom Beach

A quiet and beautiful hidden beach with soft white sand and turquoise water. Much less crowded than Patong – perfect for relaxing.

Freedom Beach
