Traditional Food in Phuket

Hover on any picture to see the information slide out

Pad Thai

Stir-fried rice noodles with egg, tofu, bean sprouts, and crushed peanuts. The most famous Thai noodle dish in the world.

Pad Thai

Tom Yum Goong ★

Hot and sour soup with fresh prawns, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves and chili. This is one of the most traditional and loved Thai dishes. Best in Phuket because of the fresh seafood.

Tom Yum Goong ★ Most Traditional

Mango Sticky Rice

Sweet sticky rice topped with fresh ripe mango and creamy coconut milk. The perfect Thai dessert that everyone loves.

Mango Sticky Rice

Massaman Curry

Mild and slightly sweet curry with potatoes, peanuts and tender meat. One of the richest and tastiest Thai curries.

Massaman Curry

Som Tum (Papaya Salad)

Spicy green papaya salad mixed with lime, chili, garlic and peanuts. Fresh, crunchy and full of flavor.

Som Tum

Thai Fried Rice

Simple but delicious fried rice with egg, vegetables and your choice of chicken, shrimp or pork. A classic everyday Thai dish.

Thai Fried Rice
